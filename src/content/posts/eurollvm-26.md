# EuroLLVM'26

...was a lot of fun. It was great to meet more people from the LLVM community, which is always a welcoming, warm, 
and inspiring one. We also presented [our work](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-S03RxeDgNE&t=1018s) on verifying LLVM's 
instruction selector, got some very helpful feedback and overall had the chance to meet and chat with many
developers actively working on instruction selection in LLVM!

Three things I want to learn about in the upcoming months: 

### floating points, inspired by [Matthias Springer's talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dG1OFwm57ko)
### fastmath flags, inspired by [Mikolaj Pirog's talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Ag4fyjcaGA)
### vectorization and its verification in LLVM, inspired by conversations with [Luke Lau](https://www.igalia.com/team/luke)
